# 🐟 Fisherman-AI

## AI Co-Pilot for Coastal Fishermen

Built for the fishing community of Karavali (Coastal Karnataka).

---

### 🎯 Problem
Small-scale fishermen struggle with:
- Complex weather advisories
- Language barriers (Kannada/Tulu/Konkani)
- Low digital literacy
- Intermittent internet connectivity

---

### 💡 Solution
Voice-first AI assistant that answers:
1. "Should I go fishing today?"
2. "Am I safe? What should I do?"
3. "How much did I earn?"
4. "How can I improve?"

---

| Layer | Technology |
