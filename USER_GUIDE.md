# 🐟 Fisherman-AI - User Guide

## How to Use the App

### 1. Voice Input (Main Feature)
- Press and **HOLD** the mic button
- Speak your question in **Kannada or English**
- Release the button
- The AI will respond with a decision

**Examples:**
- "ನಾಳೆ ಕಡಲಿಗೆ ಹೋಗಬಹುದಾ?" (Kannada)
- "Should I go fishing tomorrow?" (English)
- "Can we go fishing today?"

---

### 2. Get Fishing Decision
The AI analyzes:
- 🌤️ Weather conditions
- 💨 Wind speed
- 🌡️ Temperature
- 💰 Expected profit

**You get:**
- 🟢 GO - Conditions are favorable
- 🟡 CAUTION - Be careful
- 🔴 DON'T GO - Not safe

---

### 3. Features Overview

| Button | Function |
|--------|----------|
| 🆘 SOS | Emergency with location sharing |
| 📋 History | View past trips |
| 🎣 Catch | Log your catch via voice |
| 📊 Market | Fish prices |
| 🌊 Tide | High/Low tide times |
| 📈 Dashboard | Profit analytics |
| ⛽ Fuel | Fuel cost calculator |
| 🏛️ Schemes | Government schemes |
| 📄 Export | Download trip report |
| 🗓️ Calendar | Best fishing days |

---

### 4. Fish Identification
1. Click "Upload Fish Photo"
2. Select a fish image
3. AI identifies the fish
4. Shows: Name, Kannada name, Price

---

### 5. Offline Support
- Works without internet
- Saves data locally
- Auto-sync when online

---

### 6. Tips
- Speak clearly near the microphone
- Use simple sentences
- Check weather before going out
- Save your catches for analytics

---

### 7. Emergency
- Click 🆘 SOS
- Your location is shared
- Call 108 immediately

---

**🐟 Happy Fishing! Stay Safe!**
